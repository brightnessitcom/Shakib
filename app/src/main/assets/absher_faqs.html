Actual beneficiary means that the user is acting on their own behalf or on behalf of a direct family member registered in Absher.
You can manage all passport and resident ID services directly from the My Services tab in your Absher account.
Does the Saudi passport renewal service include family members and dependents registered in Absher?
Yes, you can renew passports for all eligible family members directly through Absher Individuals.
I have renewed my passport through the Absher platform, how can I get the new passport after completing the procedure?
Your new passport will be delivered via Saudi Post (SPL) after presenting the previous passport for verification.
How can I verify my Digital Resident ID or Passport offline without internet connection?
Open the Absher app and view your Digital ID or Passport from the home dashboard. Your QR code can be scanned by authorized officers.
How is a passport delivered after renewal through the Absher platform?
After completing the renewal request on Absher, the renewed passport is delivered via Saudi Post (SPL) to your registered National Address.
