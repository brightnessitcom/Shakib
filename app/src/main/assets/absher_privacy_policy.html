Users can visit the (Absher) platform and benefit from its electronic services safely. First: Collection of Information:

When you access or use the Absher platform and smart application, the system processes only the essential information required to verify your identity, execute your requests, and maintain the security of your digital account.

2- Digital identity and biometric data: Used solely for identity verification and secure login via Nafath and Absher services.

To help you protect your personal information, we recommend the following:
